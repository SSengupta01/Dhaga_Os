# Dhaga & Co. discovery note — 3 October 2026

**Chosen problem.** Dhaga's 34 support agents spend much of their time answering repetitive order questions while the average first response takes nine hours. A support agent that safely resolves verified read-only requests and prepares evidence-backed actions for human approval can reduce handling effort without guessing policy.

**Owner and current workaround.** Arpita, Head of CX, owns this workflow. Agents use Freshdesk, Gupshup WhatsApp, and a shared document of canned replies. The brief reports roughly 9,000 tickets weekly, 58% WISMO, and four repeatedly copied replies. That implies approximately 5,220 WISMO tickets weekly; it is a calculation, not a separately reported Dhaga metric.

**Ranked shortlist.** (1) CX support: named owner, measured volume and response baseline, clean order source. (2) Catalog delay: Vivek owns a six-to-nine-day sample-to-live cycle and missed Tuesday drops. (3) Unread reviews: Neha has 410,000 reviews but no scalable investigation process. (4) Return-reason analysis: Neha reports 31% returns and 44% in Other, but row-level reason quality and action ownership need discovery.

**Success and falsification.** Measure correct intent routing, safe-resolution rate, unsafe-send count, agent approval time, cost per case, and pipeline latency on a realistic synthetic evaluation set. Compare the existing nine-hour first-response baseline only in a later live pilot. The largest assumption is that order, policy, and carrier facts are accessible and fresh enough to resolve a useful share of cases; a sample of real tickets and lookup availability would disprove it if coverage is low.

**Evidence.** FDE Academy, *Mini Project 1: The Dhaga & Co. Engagement*, pages 2–7. The case study describes a composite company. Its figures are treated as verified for the exercise; all row-level data and illustrative policies in this repository are synthetic.
